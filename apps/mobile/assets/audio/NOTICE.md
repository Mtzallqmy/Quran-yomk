For the three files above, audio was extracted, converted to mono 44.1 kHz
Vorbis, and loudness-normalized to -18 LUFS / -2 dBTP. No phrases were
intentionally trimmed. They are field recordings and can contain ambience.
No individual muezzin's name is inferred from an unidentified recording.

`adhan.ogg` remains the existing 42-second CC0 sample by Aishatu98:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Adhan.ogg

`reminder.wav` is the original generated CC0 Tarteel notification chime.
See `manifest.json` for checksums, durations and source records.
