# Quran Yutla (قرآن يتلى) — Brand Identity & Design System

## 1. Brand Essence
«قرآن يتلى» is an original visual and acoustic brand identity.
- **Arabic Name**: قرآن يتلى
- **English Name**: Quran Yutla
- **Internal Slug**: `quran_yutla`
- **Identity Concept**: Reverence, acoustic resonance, clarity, and authentic Islamic geometric craftsmanship.

## 2. Color Tokens
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary / Deep Indigo** | `#243B6B` | Primary surface brand, app bars, and high-contrast anchors |
| **Primary Dark** | `#162746` | Deep container backgrounds and dark elevation base |
| **Teal** | `#2E9E9E` | Secondary brand color, active audio playback indicators, waveforms |
| **Copper Accent** | `#C77955` | Wooden Rihal bookstand accents, prayer markers, and focal points |
| **Pearl Background** | `#F8F6F1` | Warm light canvas for eye-safe recitation reading |
| **Night Background** | `#0E1726` | OLED-friendly deep night canvas |
| **Light Surface** | `#FFFFFF` | Light cards and sheets |
| **Dark Surface** | `#172235` | Dark cards and sheets |

## 3. Typography
- **Arabic UI**: Noto Sans Arabic / Tajawal
- **Holy Quran Text**: KFGQPC Uthman Taha Naskh / Uthmanic Hafs
- **English UI**: Inter / Noto Sans

## 4. Brand Mark Geometry
- **Composition**: An elegant geometric open Quran book (Left and Right pages on an interlocking Rihal wooden stand in Copper `#C77955`), surmounted by an ascending acoustic recitation arc and soundwaves in Teal `#2E9E9E`.
- **Scalability**: Designed to render crisply from 24×24dp toolbar icons up to 512×512dp splash/app icons.
- **Radio Variant**: Emits radiating soundwaves to symbolize live streaming broadcast.
